## 6. בטיחות נתונים (Data safety)
**Data collection and security**
- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes**
- Is all data encrypted in transit? **Yes** (HTTPS)
- Way for users to request deletion: **Yes**. Delete account URL:
```
https://skywatchcom-design.github.io/drone-kingdom/account/delete.html
```
- Account creation method: **Username and password** (and email for players 13+).

**Data types** (all: *Collected*, *not shared*, *not processed ephemerally*, *required*):

| Google category | What it is in SkyWatch | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Personal info → **Name** | Commander name (shown in game) | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → **Email address** | Only players 13+ (optional for under 13: none) | Account management (password reset) |
| Personal info → **User IDs** | Account ID | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → **Other info** | Birth month and year (age check) | App functionality, Account management |
| App activity → **Other actions** | Base, army and game progress (the save) | App functionality |

- Email is **optional** (players under 13 have none). The rest is **required**.
- Not collected: location, contacts, photos, files, device IDs, advertising ID, health, financial info,
  messages, browsing history, crash logs, diagnostics.
- Purchase history: not collected yet (purchases are not live). **Update this form when Google Play Billing goes live.**
