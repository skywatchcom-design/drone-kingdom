Privacy Policy

The game is operated by SkyWatch.

This page explains what the game SkyWatch keeps, why, and how to delete it. It is written in plain words because children play the game too.

What we keep

  • Your commander name. Other players can see it in the game.
  • Birth month and year. Only to know whether a player is under 13. We do not keep the full date.
  • Email, from age 13 only. Used only to log in and to reset the password. Players under 13 are never asked for one.
  • Password. Stored encrypted by the login service. Nobody on our side can see it.
  • Game progress: your base, buildings, resources, army, stars and missions.
  • When you agreed to the Terms of Use and this policy.

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos or phone number.
  • No open chat between players.
  • No ads and no tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children under 13

We never ask children under 13 for an email or anything that could be used to contact them. We keep only the commander name, birth month and year, and game progress. Parents can ask to see or delete their child's information at any time.

Where it is kept

Information is stored with the cloud service Supabase. The device also keeps a copy of the game and the login, so you do not need to log in every time.

Deleting your account

In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. The account and everything in it are deleted at once and for good. You can also delete it without the game on the account deletion page, or contact us and ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the version here and ask you to agree again in the game when the change matters.
